2. What the data says

✅ Famous names pull views

Nima Rumba and Prashant Tamang brought 10–50× the views of other videos. For tech: interview well-known Nepalis working in tech.

✅ Search content lasts for years

A 4-year-old karaoke video still has 38K views. People search for it. Tech tutorials work the same way.

⚠️ Most subscribers aren't watching anymore

196K subscribers but most videos get 1–5% of that. The audience has gone quiet after long gaps. You need steady uploads to win them back, not one big video.

⚠️ 1–2 hour interviews don't work as uploads

Full recorded shows get under 4K views. Keep long interviews to 20–30 min and cut the best moments into Shorts.

⚠️ Uploads come in bursts

Several videos in a week, then months of nothing. YouTube's own rule of thumb (and /yt-plan): steady beats lots.

💡 Tech is already there

The RC-500 review shows music gear and tech already fit the channel. Music + tech is your natural bridge.

  • Be open about it. Tick YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" box when a realistic AI voice or visual is used. Don't try to hide AI. If viewers find out on their own, you lose their trust, and hidden AI also risks YouTube's rules.
  • Test the clone in Nepali first. Voice-clone tools are strongest in English; Nepali quality varies. Compare the clone with your real voice before relying on it.

9. Rules we keep

Do
  • Two long videos a month, on a fixed schedule. Consistency beats volume.
  • Your face opens and closes every video. Label realistic AI voice/visuals in YouTube Studio.
  • Every video belongs to one playlist series.
  • Keep interviews 20–30 min; big names first.
  • Your own voice or face, real experience, real tests.
  • Review every video's retention before making the next.
Don't
  • No crypto trading or "earn crypto" videos. Nepal Rastra Bank has declared crypto activity illegal in Nepal; verify the current rules first.
  • No mass-produced AI videos. YouTube can stop paying channels for repetitive or inauthentic content.
  • No get-rich-quick promises.
  • No 2-hour unedited uploads.
  • No invented numbers in scripts. If we don't have the figure, leave it out.
  • No mention of your employer, and no investment or financial advice.

Open decisions

  • Language: Nepali, English, or mixed (Nepali speech + English titles, like the current channel)?
  • Presenter: you on camera, a co-host, or voice-over?
  • Series names: are "AI Sathi", "Digital Suraksha", "Kamai Online" the right names for your audience?
